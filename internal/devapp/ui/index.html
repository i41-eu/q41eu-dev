Start here

  1. Give your robot Dev as one of its apps (on m.i41.eu, or your home manager). Signed in there, this page shows your robot at once (Sign in at the top); or switch the robot to Dev (swipe up, QR, Next, Connect) and scan its QR code.
  2. New key on the robot's card below: it shows the three variables for your shell, SDEV_URL, SDEV_ROBOT, SDEV_KEY.
  3. Run the examples below here with Run, then on your computer: curl, Python or Go.

The whole way, with the robot's setup over USB: getting started. All examples: examples/.

Examples

Each runs here on your robot (Run), and the same as a script on your computer with the three variables set.

The API

Every action is POST /v1/robots/<robot>/<action> with a JSON object, and the same action is the MCP tool robot_<action> at /mcp (for AI agents). The key goes in Authorization: Bearer sdev_…. Also: GET /v1/robots, GET /v1/robots/<robot> (its state), GET /v1/robots/<robot>/events?after=<seq>&seconds=30 (waits for the next thing a person does), GET /v1/actions (this list as JSON), and /v1/openapi.json: the whole API as OpenAPI 3.1, for any OpenAPI tool or a client generator.
