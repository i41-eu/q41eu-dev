Your robot from your own programs and AI agents: show text, ask a question, light the LEDs, play a sound, hear what people do at it. One key per robot, made here; then a few lines of curl, Python, JavaScript or Go, or an AI agent over MCP.

Your robots: try every action by hand, make keys, see what people do at the robot.

Start here

  1. Give your robot Dev as one of its apps (on m.i41.eu, or your home manager). Signed in there, Your robots shows it at once (Sign in at the top); or switch the robot to Dev (swipe up, QR, Next, Connect) and scan its QR code.
  2. New key on the robot's card in Your robots: it shows the three variables for your shell, SDEV_URL, SDEV_ROBOT, SDEV_KEY.
  3. Run your first program below: here with Run, then on your computer, in the language you like.
